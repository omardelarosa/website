**Spell Pierce** is the moniker I am currently using for my solo music.  The name started on [[2025-10-20 Spell Pierce Is Born]] and I've been using it ever since.  
You can read more at: https://spellpierce.com/

It's an homage to a not-so-powerful, but useful card in Magic: The Gathering.  I play it in many modern decks.
